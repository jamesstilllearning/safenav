## A. Campus orientation and boundary analysis

**North indicator:** A circular north arrow sits in the upper-right corner with the bold label "NORTH" beneath it. The arrow points toward the **upper-right corner of the image**, so north is **diagonal** relative to the image frame — the image's top/bottom/left/right edges do not align exactly with cardinal directions. All positions below use relative image positions (upper-left, bottom edge, etc.).

**Campus boundary:** Irregular polygon.
- Bottom edge: **Congressional Road** runs horizontally just outside/along the perimeter — bottom/frontage boundary.
- Upper-right edge: the **creek** runs along the boundary.
- Right side: proposed multipurpose building, proposed CIT & ICT building, proposed material segregation facility, existing main building hall.
- Left side: existing canteen stalls, canteen roofing/floor drainage work, proposed engineering building, existing criminology building, existing medical and dental clinic.
- Upper portion: proposed dormitory, proposed multipurpose building, proposed grass quadrangle, proposed staff housing building, proposed outdoor swimming pool, proposed theater & arts building.

**Access points (two labeled):**
- Existing main gate (`existingmaingate`) — bottom edge, left of center, on Congressional Road.
- Proposed secondary access gate (`proposedSecondary Access gate`) — bottom-right corner, southeastern extent.

## C. Project status classification (per the original legend)

The bottom-left legend box ("legends") was fully read — all six entries legible:

| Color | Legend label | Status meaning |
|-------|--------------|----------------|
| Orange | PROPOSED PROJECTS | Proposed (not yet constructed) |
| Cyan | ON-GOING CONSTRUCTION / REPAIR | Under construction / being repaired |
| Dark red | COMPLETED / EXISTING BUILDING | Built and operational |
| Magenta | recently accomplished projects | Recently completed |
| Pale yellow | PROPOSED ROAD ACCESS | Proposed road access |
| Reddish orange | PROPOSED PATHWAY | Proposed pathway |

**Classification rule:** status is encoded by building footprint fill color matched to the legend — color determines status class, not label wording. No proposed facility is classified as existing/operational.

**Status conflicts flagged (do not silently resolve):**
- `PROPOSED OUTDOOR SWIMMING POOL` is labeled "PROPOSED" but filled **cyan** (ongoing construction/repair), not orange.
- `proposed Repair and Rehabilitation of ECC Food Technology Laboratory` begins with lowercase "proposed" but is rendered **magenta** (recently accomplished).
- Some labels read "EXISTING" yet appear magenta (e.g. "EXISTING MEDICAL AND DENTAL CLINIC"); label wording is used for identity, but color-based status is uncertain.
